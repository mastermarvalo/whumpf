## Safety: read this

Whumpf is a terrain planning tool. It is not a substitute for avalanche training, the CAIC forecast, or your own judgement in the field. Slope angle, aspect and elevation are computed from elevation models. They can be wrong, especially for small features like rollovers, gullies, cliffs and cornices. Terrain shown below 30° can still connect to avalanche terrain above or beside it. Forecasts, weather, snowpack, road and webcam data come from other organizations. They can be wrong, late or out of date, and may not load without signal. Maps, routes and offline maps can be incomplete or out of date. Read the full CAIC forecast before every trip. Get avalanche training, carry rescue gear, and travel with partners who carry it too. You are responsible for your decisions and your safety. Use Whumpf at your own risk.

Backcountry travel is dangerous and can kill you. You accept those risks yourself. Nothing in Whumpf tells you that a slope, route or day is safe.
